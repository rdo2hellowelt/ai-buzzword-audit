## Executive summary

Between 2025 and 2026, AI vocabulary evolved faster than the underlying systems. The shift from Generative AI to Agentic AI marks the most significant architectural transition since cloud adoption. Models moved from reactive prompting to autonomous orchestration, enabled by Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) and intent‑driven development paradigms such as Vibe Coding.
This acceleration created a new challenge: semantic saturation. The rise of low‑quality, high‑volume output ("AI Slop") forced SEO to evolve into GEO - Generative Engine Optimization.
Two domains reshaped strategic priorities:

- **Physical AI**, merging digital cognition with robotic execution.
- **Sovereign AI**, positioning compute as geopolitical leverage.

The chatbot era is behind us. Autonomous digital teammates are the new operational layer.
