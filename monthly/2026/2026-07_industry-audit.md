## Executive summary

July 2026 marks a definitive shift from LLMs as 'chat interfaces' to 'agentic operating systems.' The primary technical catalyst is 'Inference-time Scaling,' where compute is diverted from training to generation, allowing models to perform multi-step verification and 'think' before responding—effectively bridging the gap between System 1 and System 2 thinking. We observed a significant surge in 'Physical AI' discussions, driven by the integration of World Models into humanoid robotics, moving beyond static data to real-world interaction. Commercially, the narrative has pivoted toward 'Sovereign AI,' as nations and large enterprises move away from centralized API dependencies toward locally hosted, highly quantized frontier models. The era of 'Synthetic Data' hype is cooling as research on 'model collapse' and 'data poisoning' forces a return to high-quality, human-curated, and multi-modal sensory inputs. Developers are currently focused on 'Compound AI Systems'—orchestrating multiple specialized models rather than seeking a single monolithic AGI solution.
